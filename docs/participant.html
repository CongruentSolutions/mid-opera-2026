The problem

The next step

Participants have all the data. What they’re missing is the next step.

Employer match

1 in 4

employees don’t save enough to get their full match: an estimated $24B left unclaimed every year.

Contributions

39.7%

say their contribution rate is simply the default they accepted. Only about 1 in 7 contribute the IRS maximum.

Investments

1 in 3

Americans can answer three basic questions on interest, inflation and diversification. Fund tables alone don’t explain what’s working.

Portals show participants what they have. Athena helps them understand what it means.

Sources: Financial Engines, “Missing Out” (2015) · PLANSPONSOR Participant Survey (2025) · Vanguard, How America Saves · Lusardi & Mitchell

The solution

The portal that speaks up

Meet Athena, the portal that speaks up.

An AI agent built into CORE. It knows each participant’s plan and numbers, explains what matters in their own dollars, and prepares the change for them to confirm.

AreaTodayWith Athena
Employer matchThe contribution rate is shown. What it means for their match isn’t.The dollar gap from their own plan and pay, with the change ready to confirm.
ContributionsAny rate can be entered. Limit issues surface later, downstream.Plan and IRS limits checked as they type, with the highest rate they’re allowed.
InvestmentsFunds and returns are listed, without context on what needs attention.What needs attention and why, with alternatives from the plan’s own lineup.
